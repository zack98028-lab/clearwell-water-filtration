Pricing

What each system costs, and what it actually fixes.

Every price below is a real range based on standard installs in Plymouth and Canton. Your exact price depends on your test result — we confirm it in writing before any work starts.

01 — Most common

Water softener

Removes calcium and magnesium — the minerals that cause scale on faucets, spots on dishes, and premature water heater wear. Fixes the single most common complaint in Plymouth and Canton, where GLWA municipal water typically runs 7–13 grains per gallon of hardness.

Good fit if: you're seeing white buildup on fixtures, dry skin/hair after showers, or your water heater is failing early.

Installed price

$2,000–3,500

Owned outright — no rental agreement, no repossession clause. Includes permit and inspection.

Ongoing: salt delivery ~$10–30/visit

02 — Taste & odor

Whole-home filtration

Reduces chlorine taste and smell, plus sediment, across every tap in the house. This does not remove hardness — it's a separate fix from a softener, and some homes want both.

Good fit if: your water smells or tastes like a swimming pool, or you're seeing cloudiness/grit at the tap.

Installed price

$1,000–4,500

Typical job lands around $2,000–2,500 for a standard single-family home.

Ongoing: cartridge swap ~$50–150/year

03 — Drinking water only

Under-sink reverse osmosis

A separate, smaller system installed under the kitchen sink only — not whole-home. Pushes water through a fine membrane for noticeably better-tasting drinking and cooking water, and reduces trace contaminants a softener won't touch.

Good fit if: you want the best possible drinking water, or your municipal water tested with elevated trace contaminants.

Installed price

$300–2,500

Basic units start around $300; premium tankless systems run toward $2,500. Average installed cost is $1,100–1,300.

Ongoing: membrane swap every 3–5 years

04 — Salem Township & well homes

Well water treatment

Private wells aren't regulated or tested by any city. Common issues in this area: iron/manganese staining, sulfur (rotten-egg) odor, and hardness. Some problems — coliform bacteria, nitrates, arsenic — are invisible and only show up on a real test.

We test first, every time. Coliform and hardness are part of the free basic test. Nitrate, arsenic, and full mineral panels are available as an add-on.

Installed price

$3,000–8,000+

Bundled system — typically sediment filter + iron/manganese filter + softener. UV disinfection added if bacteria is confirmed.

Note: we don't touch the well itself (pump, casing) — that's a licensed well contractor, and we'll refer you to one if that's the actual issue.

Not sure which one you need? That's what the test is for.
