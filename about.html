About

We started this because the industry doesn't tell you enough.

Most water treatment companies quote over the phone, sell you a rental you never own, and don't explain what's actually wrong with your water. We built Clearwell to do the opposite of all three.

What we test, not guess

Every job starts with an actual on-site test — not a sales pitch. Grains per gallon, iron, coliform bacteria for well homes. If your water tests clean and you don't need anything, we tell you that.

What you own, not rent

Flat, published pricing. When the job's done, the system is yours — no contract, no monthly fee, no repossession clause buried in fine print.

Where we work

Plymouth, Canton, and the well-water homes west of Napier Road.

We're based in Plymouth and stay close to home on purpose — it's how we keep response times short and actually know the water in the neighborhoods we serve. Salem Township and the well-water homes west of Napier Road get particular attention: private wells aren't tested or regulated by anyone, so that's where a real water test matters most.

We're not a national franchise, and we're not going to try to be everywhere at once. We'd rather be genuinely good in one area than thin across ten.

How we talk to you

Specific, not vague. Honest, even when it costs us a sale.

We'll tell you it's iron, not "minerals." We'll tell you the number, not "pretty hard." And if your test comes back clean, we'll tell you that you don't need the premium system — because the whole point of testing first is that the answer isn't decided before we show up.
